Ignacio Lepe

Pre-Doctoral Fellow · Tobin Center for Economic Policy, Yale University

I am a Pre-Doctoral Fellow at the Tobin Center for Economic Policy and the Department of Economics at Yale University, under the supervision of Professor Christopher Neilson. My research interests include labor economics, the economics of education, and market design, with a focus on matching mechanisms in education markets. I hold a Bachelor’s and a Master’s degree in Economics from the University of Chile.

I am also affiliated with ConsiliumBots, where we design education technology and run randomized controlled trials to improve access to education across the United States and Latin America.
