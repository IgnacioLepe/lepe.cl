Generative AI Use and Student Achievement: Evidence from PISA 2025

Ignacio Lepe & Dante Contreras · Working paper, 2026 · Revise and resubmit at Economics of Education Review (Short Communication)

Abstract

PISA 2025 is the first cycle of the assessment to ask 15-year-olds how they use AI chatbots for schoolwork, where 54.2 percent of students in OECD countries report at least weekly use. Comparing schoolmates with similar backgrounds, we find that more AI use is associated with lower achievement. Among students with the same level of use, those who use it more for research and learning score higher than those who use it more for summarizing and drafting. The data are a single cross-section of self-reports, so these associations cannot show whether AI helps or harms learning.
